Privacy Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

Kadayo is a wellness and accountability app for doing it together with friends. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it lives, and the choices you have. The short version: your logs belong to you, nothing is public, we don’t run ads, and we never sell your data.

Your account

You sign in with Apple. We receive a unique identifier, the name you choose to share, and your email (which may be Apple’s private relay address). Your profile (display name, @username and preferences) is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, in the United States.

What you log

Weights, measurements, progress photos, meals with their photos and videos, water, practices, workouts and runs, check-ins and goals are stored on your device and with your account, so they survive a new phone. Photos and videos go to private storage; links to them are signed and expire. Nothing here is visible to anyone else unless you share it.

Sharing controls

Everything you log is private by default. When you post something, you choose who sees it: Just me, Team chat only, Friends, or Friends and team chat. A meetup invite goes only to the people you pick. There are no public profiles and no way for strangers to see your content. Blocking someone makes you invisible to each other. In a challenge, your check-ins are visible to your team: your focus number and how it changed, how your practices went, the note you choose to share, and a progress photo if you add one.

Step challenges

If you join a challenge with a steps goal, your daily step count for the days of that challenge is shared with your team, so the team total and the standings work. In a Beat your average challenge, your steps from the two weeks before it starts are shared too; teammates see only your change, never those numbers.

Apple Health

With your permission, the app reads workouts, distance, routes, steps, heart rate, active energy, sleep and mindful minutes from Apple Health to fill in your logs. Health data is used only for features you use, is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never sold. It leaves your device only when you share a workout, or as your daily steps in a step challenge you join.

AI features

Kadayo’s AI features (meal photos, Say it, recipe links, nutrition labels and Ask Kadayo’s calorie suggestion) send what you submit through our server to Anthropic’s Claude API to produce an estimate. For Ask Kadayo that includes your answers and, if you’ve logged them, your weight and goal direction. The app asks before your first use. Anthropic processes this only to return the answer, under terms that don’t allow it to train its models on your data. Say it turns your speech into text with Apple’s speech recognition before anything is sent. Barcode scans and food searches query Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central without any personal information attached.

Location

The app uses your location only while you record a run, walk or ride, to measure distance and pace and draw your route. Recording continues if you leave the app mid-run and stops when you finish. Routes are stored with the workout and shared only if you share it.

Apple Calendar

If you turn on calendar sync, Kadayo adds your meetups and check-in days to a calendar on your phone and shows your calendar’s upcoming events in the Calendar tab. Your calendar events stay on your phone and are never sent to our server.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store your device’s notification token so we can tell you about team chat, check-ins, friend requests, cheers and meetup invites. You can turn notifications off any time in your iPhone’s Settings.

Purchases

Kadayo Plus is bought through Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells our server which plan you have and when it renews, ends or is refunded, linked to your account.

Team chat

Messages you send in a team are stored with that team and readable by its members, including people who later leave, so the conversation they were part of stays intact. There are no private one-to-one messages in Kadayo.

Reports

If you report a person or a post, we store the report, your reason, and a copy of what you reported, so it can be reviewed.

What we don’t do

No advertising, no ad tracking, no tracking you across other apps or websites, and no selling or renting your information. Ever.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account any time from Profile, Delete account. This permanently removes your account and server-side data, and clears the app’s data on your device. There is no recovery after deletion. Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a Kadayo Plus subscription; cancel it in your iPhone’s Settings.

Children

Kadayo is not directed at anyone under 16, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially, the app will tell you. Questions or requests: support@kadayo.app.
