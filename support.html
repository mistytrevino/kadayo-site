Cancel Kadayo Plus

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Kadayo. Or in Kadayo, Profile, Kadayo Plus, Plan.

Delete your account

In Kadayo, Profile, Delete account. It removes your account and your data for good. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, so cancel that first.
